About SMJV

106+ Years of Service & Excellence

Established on June 18, 1915 under the blessings of Acharya Bhagwant Shri Vijay Vallabh Suriswarji Maharaj Saheb, SMJV is one of India's oldest Jain educational trusts — providing hostels, financial support, and spiritual grounding to thousands.

"જીવનમાં આત્મ કલ્યાણ અને લોકકલ્યાણની ભાવનાનો પુણ્ય સંગમ..."

— A blessed confluence of self-welfare and community service
