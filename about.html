About SMJV

Serving the Jain community with quality education, hostel facilities, and financial support since 1915.

Our Journey

A Legacy of Over a Century

1915

Founded on June 18, 1915, under the blessings of Acharya Bhagwant Shri Vijay Vallabh Suriswarji Maharaj Saheb.

1920-1950

Expanded educational initiatives, established schools and pathshalas across Gujarat and Maharashtra.

1970-1990

Launched hostel facilities in Mumbai, Vadodara, and Ahmedabad for working professionals and students.

2000-Present

12 hostels across 7 cities, interest-free financial aid, and global alumni network across 30+ countries.

Our Core Values

Grounded in Jain Philosophy

🙏

Ahimsa (Non-Violence)

Promoting peaceful living and compassion towards all beings.

📚

Vidya Daan (Education)

Providing quality education and knowledge to all deserving students.

🤝

Seva (Service)

Selfless service to the community and humanity at large.

🕊️

Aparigraha (Non-Possession)

Encouraging simple living and sharing resources with others.
