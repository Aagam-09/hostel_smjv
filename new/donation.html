Adding Value Through Grounded Values

Our Mission: To transform new-age life by infusing age-old values based on spiritual and practical wisdom, which eventually inspires the whole community to progress. This inspiration is like a sacred essence that flows through the divine medium of Parab.

Shri Mahavira Jaina Vidyalaya has been blessed with the embodiment of practical education and religious values since the year 1915 by the respected Yugdrashta Acharyashri Vijay Vallabhsurishwarji Maharaj Saheb, our guiding force at SMJV.

"श्री विजय वल्लभ सूरीश्वरजी महाराज साहेब के आशीर्वाद से शताब्दी की सेवा"
